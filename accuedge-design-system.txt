1. BRAND & IDENTITY
================================================================

App Name: AccuEdge
Tagline: AI Racing Intelligence
Logo treatment: "Accu" in white or navy, "Edge" in gold (#c9a84c)
Font size for logo: 26–40px, font-weight 900, letter-spacing -0.5px

Brand personality: Premium, analytical, data-driven. Like a
Bloomberg terminal crossed with a high-end sports app. Dark,
confident, gold accents signal exclusivity.

3. TYPOGRAPHY
================================================================

Font Family: 'Outfit', sans-serif
Google Fonts import weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700
Applied to: body, all buttons (font-family: inherit)

Base font size: 16px (html), responsive, no change below 600px

--- SCALE (most common sizes used) ---

28–30px  weight 700–900   Hero/slider horse names, large headings
22px     weight 900        Slider horse name (scaled)
20px     weight 800        Odds in hero, large prices
18px     weight 800        Section sub-labels like "Today's Analysis"
17px     weight 700        Venue card names, pick card horse names
16px     weight 700        Race title in expanded card header
15px     weight 700        Section nav buttons, body CTAs
14px     weight 500–700    Body text, fixture rows, runner names
13px     weight 500–700    Standard buttons, labels, analysis text
12px     weight 400–600    Metadata, sub-labels, detail lines
11px     weight 400–600    Small metadata (going, runners, distance)
10px     weight 600–700    Badge text, uppercase labels
9px      weight 700        Micro labels, AI EDGE badge text
8px      weight 700        AI EDGE badge text (smallest usage)
7px      weight 700        Smallest label (compact slider context)

--- TEXT TRANSFORMS ---

Uppercase + letter-spacing: used for section eyebrows, badge labels
Letter-spacing values: .01em (subtle) up to .2em (eyebrow labels)

--- FONT WEIGHTS IN USE ---

300  — light (rare, subtle body text)
400  — regular (body, metadata)
500  — medium (nav links, fixture rows)
600  — semibold (section titles, odds)
700  — bold (headings, CTAs, venue names)
800  — extrabold (slider title, odds highlights)
900  — black (horse names in slider, logo)

14. MOBILE-FIRST RULES
================================================================

Breakpoint: 600px
Below 600px:
  - Bottom nav becomes visible (fixed, z-index: 300)
  - Main content gets 80px bottom padding for nav clearance
  - Top nav height: 56–60px
  - Slip panel goes full-width near bottom
  - Font sizes scale up slightly for tap targets
  - All CTAs are full-width stacked (never side-by-side)
  - Tap targets minimum 44px height
  - All carousels: scroll-snap, -webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch
  - Scrollbars hidden: scrollbar-width: none

Touch interactions:
  - Swipe left/right on race cards to move between races
  - Swipe triggers at dx > 40px and dx > dy (not a scroll)
  - All swipe containers: touchstart + touchend listeners
  - Element cloned before re-attaching listeners to prevent accumulation

VENUE CARD EXPAND:

  3. Opens correct venue
  4. Pre-selects the exact race by time index
  5. Scrolls to meeting list

SLIDER → RACE NAVIGATION:
  "Go to Race →" CTA on slider does same race navigation as above

ADD TO SLIP:
  Any price button or "Add to Slip" CTA adds horse + odds to slip panel
  Slip badge count updates
  Slip toggle button visible fixed bottom-right

  On login: overlay hides, app content appears
